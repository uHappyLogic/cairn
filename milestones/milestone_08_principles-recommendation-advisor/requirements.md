# Milestone 8: Principles As Recommendation Advisor

## Goal

Convert the project-wide answering principles from an auto-answer engine into a recommendation advisor. Remove the redundant /try-answer-all-questions-by-principle skill and its try-answer-question-by-principle agent, and make the shared recommendation core (shared/recommend-procedure.md, used by both /recommend-all-open-questions's agent and /discuss-open-question) principle-aware: when a confirmed principle from milestones/answer_decision_principles.md bears on a question, it drives and is cited in the recommended option, otherwise recommendations are produced as today. The principle store's data and format are unchanged — only its consumer pointer moves. Scrub the now-dead auto-answer provenance vocabulary throughout (Principle-based-answer: subject, Answer-Principle: trailer, capture's exclusion grep, the revert-then-re-answer correction wording), and sync CLAUDE.md, README.md, and the principle-store header to the new model.

## Relevant starting state

### Principle store and its current consumer wiring

`milestones/answer_decision_principles.md` is a single project-wide file at the `milestones/` root (above any one milestone), holding user-confirmed answering principles. Each entry is a `### <Short Title>` subsection whose body is a generalizable **keep/eliminate directive** (applied to the candidate answers of a future question) plus an optional `*Origin:*` line; presence means confirmed, no status field. It currently holds four principles. Its **header prose names `try-answer-all-questions-by-principle` as the consumer** ("Each is a reusable keep/eliminate directive that `try-answer-all-questions-by-principle` can apply…" and "written **only** by `capture-milestone-principle-updates`"). The data and per-entry format are what this milestone keeps unchanged — only the consumer pointer moves.

### The auto-answer sweep to be removed

`skills/try-answer-all-questions-by-principle/SKILL.md` is the autonomous orchestrator: it requires a clean tree, gathers open/deferred questions most-significant-first, dispatches the read-only `agents/try-answer-question-by-principle.md` subagent per question (candidate elimination → verdict with a unique survivor + load-bearing principles), records each survivor via `shared/answer-procedure.md`, and commits one auto-answer per commit — subject `Principle-based-answer: <Short Title>`, one repeated `Answer-Principle: <Short Title>` trailer per principle. Both the skill and the agent are the two files to delete. The subagent is read-only; all mutation/commit lives in the orchestrator.

### The recommendation core and its two wrappers (the upgrade target)

`shared/recommend-procedure.md` is the execution-neutral analytical core — ground in live code → enumerate 2–4 honest alternatives (what-it-is / advantage / drawback) → recommend one with a tie-break. It is **not currently principle-aware**: it never reads the principle store. Two wrappers consume it via `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}`: (a) the `discuss-open-question` skill runs it inline in conversation; (b) `agents/recommend-open-question.md` runs it read-only per question and renders the result as the `>`-blockquote sub-block, whose last line is the liftable `> **Recommendation:** <chosen option> — <rationale>` anchor. `skills/recommend-all-open-questions/SKILL.md` orchestrates that agent across all questions (mutate-but-do-not-commit) and repeatedly describes itself as the "argument-free twin of `/try-answer-all-questions-by-principle`" — comparison anchors that break when the twin is deleted.

### Principle capture (writer — retained, lightly scrubbed)

`skills/capture-milestone-principle-updates/SKILL.md` stays the sole writer of the principle store; its manual-answer → principle learning input is untouched. It walks `git log --grep='^Manual-answer: ' -- <MILESTONE_DIR>/requirements.md` and **excludes any commit carrying an `Answer-Principle:` trailer** (the sweep's signature) as circular. Once the sweep is gone nothing emits that trailer, so the exclusion becomes vacuous; its step-5 report also points captured principles at `/try-answer-all-questions-by-principle`. Both references are part of the scrub.

### Retired-vocabulary blast radius (thorough scrub)

The now-dead auto-answer provenance vocabulary appears in these live files beyond the two deleted ones. `Principle-based-answer:` / `Answer-Principle:`: `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `skills/answer-open-question/SKILL.md` (documents the trailer's *absence* as the manual-vs-sweep discriminator), `skills/capture-milestone-principle-updates/SKILL.md`, `skills/answer-open-question-with-recommendation/SKILL.md` + `agents/answer-open-question-with-recommendation.md` (the `Recommendation-answer:` subject is defined by contrast against both). The `try-answer` name also appears in `shared/answer-procedure.md`, `skills/answer-all-open-questions-with-recommendation/SKILL.md`, and `skills/recommend-all-open-questions/SKILL.md`. The revert-then-re-answer correction wording lives in `README.md` and `CLAUDE.md` (and the deleted skill).

### CLAUDE.md / README.md sync surface

`CLAUDE.md` carries the full skill roster, pipeline block, layout, and a dense set of invariants that describe the sweep, its read-only-subagent design, its commit rules, and its relationship to capture and the recommend twin. `README.md` mirrors this in the skill reference plus the *Iterating milestone requirements* Mermaid diagram (which has a sweep node/edge). Both need syncing to the recommendation-advisor model — including the principle store's new consumer story.

## Decisions

## Open questions

> **Open question — Principle citation placement:** The `recommend-open-question` agent renders the recommendation block ending in the `> **Recommendation:** <chosen option> — <rationale>` anchor, which `/answer-open-question-with-recommendation` lifts **verbatim** as the recorded answer — and recorded `## Decisions` prose is deliberately provenance-free/citation-free. If a confirmed principle is to be *cited* in the block, where does the citation live so provenance does not leak into `## Decisions` on lift? (e.g. a separate `> **Applied principle:** <Short Title>` line that is *not* part of the lifted anchor, vs. baking "per principle X" into the anchor rationale that then flows into the decision.)
>
> **Alternatives:**
> - **Separate `> **Applied principle:** <Short Title>` line, placed above the `> **Recommendation:**` anchor** — the citation is its own labeled `>` line inside the contiguous run, distinct from the anchor. *Advantage:* needs zero change to the lift machinery (`answer-with-recommendation-procedure.md` strips only the anchor line, so this line is invisible to it) and keeps `## Decisions` citation-free by construction, while `answer-procedure.md` step 4 clears the whole run on answer so nothing lingers. *Drawback:* adds one more labeled line to the block's shape, and its placement is constrained — it must sit above the anchor so the "anchor is always the last line" rendering invariant holds.
> - **Bake "per principle X" into the anchor rationale** — the provenance rides inside the `> **Recommendation:** … — <rationale>` text itself. *Advantage:* no new line type; the citation travels with the reasoning in the single line a reader most looks at. *Drawback:* the anchor is lifted verbatim into `## Decisions`, so provenance leaks directly into prose that is deliberately citation-free/provenance-free — the exact failure the question guards against.
> - **Cite the principle only inside the driven alternative's bullet** — fold "supported by principle X" into that option's advantage field rather than a dedicated line. *Advantage:* introduces no new line type and stays out of the lift. *Drawback:* buries the provenance in one bullet and blurs advisory reasoning against provenance, making it non-obvious and non-greppable versus an explicit labeled line.
>
> **Recommendation:** Separate `> **Applied principle:**` line placed above the anchor — it is the only placement that keeps `## Decisions` citation-free by construction with zero change to the verbatim-lift mechanism, since the lift keys off exactly the `> **Recommendation:**` anchor and ignores every other line in the run.

> **Open question — Principle supply vs. core neutrality:** `shared/recommend-procedure.md` is deliberately execution-neutral and path-free — it does not even resolve `<MILESTONE_DIR>` (the caller supplies the QUESTION). Making it principle-aware, does the core name the fixed store path `milestones/answer_decision_principles.md` directly, or does it take the confirmed principles as a caller-supplied input (like QUESTION), with each wrapper (`discuss-open-question` inline, the `recommend-open-question` agent in isolation) resolving and reading the store itself?
>
> **Alternatives:**
> - **Core names the fixed store path directly** — `shared/recommend-procedure.md`'s grounding step gains an instruction to read `milestones/answer_decision_principles.md` itself and fold the confirmed principles into the recommendation. *Advantage:* consistent with the core's existing grounding behavior, which already reads live `requirements.md` and source files directly rather than receiving their contents as inputs — and because the store is a fixed path above any milestone, naming it needs no `<MILESTONE_DIR>` resolution, so the caller-owns-milestone-resolution contract stays intact; the principle-aware read lives once in the single source of truth. *Drawback:* embeds a concrete repo file path into a core billed as path-free, so a future relocation of the store touches the shared core rather than only the wrappers.
> - **Wrappers read the store and pass principles in as input (like QUESTION)** — the core adds a PRINCIPLES input alongside QUESTION; `discuss-open-question` and the `recommend-open-question` agent each resolve, read, and forward the store contents. *Advantage:* keeps the core strictly input-driven and path-free, mirroring `shared/answer-procedure.md`'s SHORT TITLE + ANSWER contract where wrappers own all file access. *Drawback:* duplicates the identical fixed-path store-read into both wrappers, and is internally inconsistent — the core's own grounding step still reads `requirements.md`/source directly, so principles alone would arrive as pre-read input while everything else is read in place.
>
> **Recommendation:** Core names the fixed store path directly — the store is a fixed, milestone-independent path, so naming it preserves the only path-freedom that matters (caller-owned `<MILESTONE_DIR>` resolution) while staying consistent with grounding's existing read-live-files-directly behavior and avoiding duplicated read logic across both wrappers.

> **Deferred — Principle drive strength:** How a bearing principle combines with ordinary merit-based recommendation when it does not fully determine a unique pick (drive the choice when a principle is decisive; merely inform/narrow when it only eliminates some candidates). Better tuned against real questions while authoring the procedure text.
>
> **Alternatives:**
> - **Hard eliminate-gate (binding constraint)** — treat a bearing principle as a filter applied before merit: drop every candidate it eliminates, recommend only from the survivors, let the existing tie-break decide among them, and cite the principle when it removed the pick's rivals. *Advantage:* honors the store's directives as the user-confirmed constraints they already are, and yields the decisive→drive / partial→narrow / none→merit gradient for free from one uniform rule with no new machinery. *Drawback:* rigid — a principle that mis-generalizes to this question can force out the genuinely best option, leaning entirely on the human reviewer to catch it.
> - **Weighted advisory factor** — treat a bearing principle as a strong thumb on the scale that merit can still override, cited whenever it influenced the pick. *Advantage:* flexible enough to recommend a principle-eliminated candidate when merit is compelling, catching mis-bearing principles automatically. *Drawback:* dilutes the authority of confirmed directives, blurs whether a pick was principle-driven or merit-driven, and reintroduces exactly the subjective weighing the keep/eliminate store was built to remove — hard to render as crisp procedure prose.
> - **Graduated drive-vs-inform (the deferred framing)** — write two explicit modes: a decisive principle "drives" the pick, a partial one merely "informs/narrows." *Advantage:* matches the question's original wording and reads intuitively. *Drawback:* "drive" and "inform" are two labels for the same eliminate-then-merit mechanic, so it spends extra prose on a distinction with no behavioral difference and invites ambiguity about what "inform" concretely does.
>
> **Recommendation:** Hard eliminate-gate — apply a bearing principle as a binding pre-merit filter (recommend only from survivors, existing tie-break decides among them, cite the principle when it eliminated the rivals), because the store's already-confirmed keep/eliminate directives deserve constraint-strength authority and this single rule produces the full decisive/partial/none gradient with no new machinery, while the always-present human answerer is the escape valve for a genuinely mis-bearing principle.

> **Deferred — Manual-vs-sweep discriminator scrub:** `skills/answer-open-question/SKILL.md` documents the *absence* of an `Answer-Principle:` trailer as the discriminator between manual and sweep auto-answer commits; with the sweep gone that counterpart disappears. Whether to reword it (e.g. against `Recommendation-answer:` as the only other answer subject) or drop the discriminator note entirely — a mechanical wording call best settled while editing.
>
> **Alternatives:**
> - **Drop the discriminator note entirely** — delete the orphaned "no `Answer-Principle:` trailer / its absence marks this a manual vs. auto-answer" sentence and the sweep-mirroring clauses, leaving no replacement; the `Manual-answer:` subject and its surviving `git log --grep='^Manual-answer: '` capture rationale stand on their own. *Advantage:* truest to a dead-vocabulary scrub — it removes what became vacuous when the sweep and the only `Answer-Principle:` emitter disappeared, and invents no new framing. *Drawback:* a reader comparing the two surviving answer subjects (`Manual-answer:` vs `Recommendation-answer:`) gets no in-file sentence spelling out how they differ.
> - **Reword the discriminator against `Recommendation-answer:`** — keep a discriminator sentence but repoint it, e.g. "the `Manual-answer:` subject (not `Recommendation-answer:`) is what marks a capture-harvested manual answer." *Advantage:* preserves an explicit "how this subject differs from its sibling" note for future readers. *Drawback:* manufactures a discriminator the design does not need — the two subjects are already separated solely by the `^Manual-answer:` grep anchor, so a trailer-style "discriminator" note restates existing machinery and risks reading as newly-invented, cutting against a clean scrub.
>
> **Recommendation:** Drop the discriminator note entirely — with the sweep and its `Answer-Principle:` trailer gone there is nothing left to discriminate against, and the `^Manual-answer:` capture grep already carries every fact the subject needs; a repointed discriminator would invent framing the scrub is meant to remove, not relocate.
